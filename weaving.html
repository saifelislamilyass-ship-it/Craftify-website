Entry Experience

Weaving

Interlace color and texture on a small loom — a calming, hands-on introduction to traditional Moroccan weaving.

What to expect

  1. 1

    The world of Moroccan textiles

    Begin with the fascinating world of Moroccan weaving and rug making. Discover how fibers, colors, motifs, and techniques have been transformed into textiles that can carry practical, decorative, social, and cultural meanings.

  2. 2

    From fiber to tradition

    Meet the materials and understand how a textile begins. Discover the role of the loom, warp, and weft, while exploring how weaving knowledge has traditionally been learned through observation, repetition, and transmission between generations of makers.

  3. 3

    Learn the rhythm of the loom

    Experience the fundamental gestures of weaving yourself. Learn how threads intersect, how tension is controlled, and how repetition gradually transforms individual fibers into a structured textile.

  4. 4

    Weave your own composition

    Choose your palette, textures, and materials and begin building your piece. Experiment with combinations and patterns while discovering the patience and rhythm required to create something row by row.

  5. 5

    From thread to story

    Leave with your creation and a new appreciation for the time, technique, creativity, and inherited knowledge behind Moroccan textile traditions.
