Entry Experience

Pottery

Shape, throw, and glaze a piece of your own — a hands-on introduction to Moroccan ceramics, no experience required.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Welcome & introduction

    Meet your facilitator and get a short overview of the clay and tools you'll be using.

  2. 2

    Centering the clay

    Learn to center and open the clay, guided one-on-one every step of the way.

  3. 3

    Shaping your piece

    Shape your own bowl, cup, or small vessel with hands-on support from your facilitator.

  4. 4

    Finishing touches

    Add texture or simple detailing before your piece is set aside to dry.

  5. 5

    Pickup & next steps

    Your piece is fired over the following days — we'll let you know when it's ready to collect.
