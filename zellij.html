Entry Experience

Zellij

Cut, arrange, and set your own geometric tile piece — a hands-on introduction to one of Morocco's most iconic crafts.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Step into the world of Zellij

    Begin with the story behind one of Morocco's most recognizable crafts. Discover how geometry, architecture, color, and skilled handwork come together in zellij, and how this knowledge has been transmitted through generations of maâlems and artisans.

  2. 2

    Read the patterns

    Before creating, we learn to look at zellij differently. Explore the logic behind its geometric compositions, discover traditional shapes and patterns, and understand how individual fragments come together to form intricate designs.

  3. 3

    Meet the artisan's gestures

    Get hands-on with the materials and tools of the craft. Discover how zellij tiles are traditionally shaped and refined, then experience some of the fundamental gestures behind a practice built on precision, patience, and years of transmitted know-how.

  4. 4

    Compose your own Zellij

    Choose your colors and shapes and begin assembling your composition. Guided throughout the process, you'll experience how geometry gradually transforms individual pieces into one harmonious pattern.

  5. 5

    Take a piece of the story with you

    Complete your creation while reflecting on the techniques and heritage behind it. You'll leave not only with your own zellij piece, but with a deeper understanding of the craftsmanship, patience, and knowledge carried within it.
